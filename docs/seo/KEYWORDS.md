# Amar Circular: keyword research (26 Nov 2026)

Market: Bangladesh. Volumes are Google searches per month in Bangladesh (OpenSEO / DataForSEO, 26 Nov 2026).
KD = keyword difficulty 0-100 (lower is easier). Phrases marked "autocomplete" come from Google's own suggestions
(real searches, no volume figure).

## The opportunity

People search for Bangladesh Bank circulars **by department and number** ("brpd circular 7 2025",
"bfiu circular 26"), and every one of these has low difficulty (mostly under 20). The only strong rivals are
bb.org.bd (no summaries), news sites and regalert.today. Amar Circular already has a page per circular in exactly
the shape people type, so the job is: get indexed, cover the most-searched circulars with summaries, and build
links.

## Not ours (skip)

Job searches dominate the generic phrases: "bangladesh bank job circular" (4,400), "ad circular" (2,400),
"apply online", "admit card", "exam date". Bank names (brac bank, islami bank) are navigational. Interest rate
and exchange rate "today" searches want live numbers, not circulars.

## Patterns worth building on later

- `<dept> circular <number> <year>` and `... pdf` / `... pdf download` / `... bangla`: one page per circular,
  title starts with the exact reference ("BRPD Circular No. 16/2022: ...") plus the Bangla reference
  ("বিআরপিডি সার্কুলার নং ১৬/২০২২"). Done.
- `<dept> master circular` and `<topic> master circular` (SME, EDF, cash incentive, loan classification,
  rescheduling, BFIU 26): evergreen, high value. 57 of 58 master circulars in the library are summarised.
- "summary of bfiu circular 26", "bfiu circular 26 summary": people want exactly what we produce.
- Other regulators people search the same way: BSEC circular (170), IDRA, NBR. A future expansion.
